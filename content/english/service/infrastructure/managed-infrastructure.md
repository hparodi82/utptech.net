### Keep the platform healthy after launch

Building infrastructure is only the beginning. We provide ongoing management for the systems behind your applications so problems are found early, changes are documented, and recovery is planned before it is needed.

Our managed service focuses on the infrastructure we know deeply: **Linux systems, private cloud platforms, containers, Kubernetes, storage, networking, monitoring, and backups**.

#### What we manage

- System health, capacity, and availability
- Updates and planned maintenance
- Backup monitoring and recovery testing
- Container and application platform operations
- Network and service troubleshooting
- Documentation and change tracking
- Infrastructure planning as your needs evolve

Engagements are scoped around your actual environment and operational needs. We do not sell a generic bundle filled with services you will never use.
