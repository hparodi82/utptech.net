### Cloud capability on infrastructure you control

We design and operate compact private cloud environments for organizations that need reliable application hosting without handing every workload and recurring cost to a public-cloud provider.

Our builds combine **Canonical MicroCloud, LXD, Linux, software-defined networking, and dependable storage** into a platform sized for your organization. The result is a practical foundation for virtual machines, containers, internal services, development environments, and future growth.

#### What we provide

- Private-cloud planning and architecture
- MicroCloud and LXD deployment
- Workload migration from legacy servers or virtualization platforms
- Network segmentation and secure service exposure
- Storage, backup, and recovery design
- Monitoring, documentation, and lifecycle planning

Every environment is built to be understandable and maintainable—not a black box that only a vendor can operate.
