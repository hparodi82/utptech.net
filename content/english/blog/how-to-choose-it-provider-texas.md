Choosing an IT service provider is one of the most important technology decisions your business will make. The right partner keeps your systems running, your data secure, and your team productive. The wrong one leaves you with recurring problems and no accountability.

## What to Look For

**Local presence matters**: A provider based in Texas understands your market, can arrive on-site quickly, and builds a real relationship with your team — not just a remote ticket queue.

**Proactive, not reactive**: Ask any prospective provider how they prevent problems, not just how they fix them. Managed IT is about monitoring, patching, and planning — not waiting for your call.

**Transparent pricing**: Avoid providers with long contracts loaded with hidden fees. Look for clear, all-inclusive monthly pricing per user or per device.

**Vendor agnostic**: Your provider should recommend what is best for your business, not what earns them the highest margin. Ask about their vendor relationships upfront.

**Security-first mindset**: Any provider worth hiring should be able to articulate your security posture, not just your uptime. Ask what happens if ransomware hits you tomorrow.

## Red Flags to Watch For

- No clear SLA or response time guarantees
- They only show up when something breaks
- Vague answers to security questions
- No client references in your industry
- Pushing proprietary hardware that locks you in

## Questions to Ask During Evaluation

1. What does your monitoring cover and how often do you check alerts?
2. How do you handle after-hours emergencies?
3. What is your average response time for a critical issue?
4. Can you provide references from businesses similar to mine?
5. How will you help us meet compliance requirements?
