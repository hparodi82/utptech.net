If your business is still running on a traditional PBX phone system, you may be paying more than you need to — and missing features your competitors already have. Here is how VoIP stacks up.

## What Is VoIP?

Voice over Internet Protocol (VoIP) routes phone calls over your internet connection instead of traditional copper telephone lines. Modern VoIP systems offer far more than just calls — think video conferencing, mobile apps, voicemail-to-email, call recording, and detailed analytics.

## Cost Comparison

Traditional landlines typically run $30–$50 per line per month, plus long-distance charges and hardware maintenance costs. VoIP systems generally run $15–$30 per user per month, include unlimited calling in the US and Canada, and require minimal on-site hardware.

For a 10-person office, the savings can easily exceed $3,000 per year.

## Feature Advantages of VoIP

- **Remote-ready**: Staff can use their business number from anywhere — home, car, or client site
- **Auto-attendant**: Route callers to the right department without a receptionist
- **Voicemail to email**: Receive voicemails as audio files in your inbox
- **Call analytics**: See who called, when, how long, and whether it was answered
- **Easy scaling**: Add or remove lines in minutes, not days

## When Traditional Phone Still Makes Sense

If your internet connection is unreliable or your business is in a rural area with inconsistent service, traditional lines may provide better call quality. However, for most Texas businesses with solid internet, VoIP is the clear choice.
